## 🌍 The React Kingdom - Geographic Overview

The React Kingdom spans diverse terrain: from the bustling Four Quarters at its heart, to floating sky structures above, mountain ranges to the north and west, underground realms carved beneath, and coastal regions opening to the digital seas. Each region's geography reflects its purpose - mountains for challenging performance work, depths for testing and type safety, skies for elevated concepts, seas for global deployment.

### Major Geographic Features:
- **Four Quarters** (Ground Level) - The kingdom's heart where most citizens live and learn
- **Central Nexus** - Crystalline plaza where all roads converge
- **Sky Realm** (Above) - Floating structures connected by sky bridges
- **Mountain Ranges** (North & West) - Peaks, caverns, and floating sanctuaries
- **Underground Realms** (Beneath) - Carved chambers for testing and quality
- **Coastal Region** (South) - Harbors, docks, and maritime deployment
- **Beyond Borders** - Allied Kingdoms, Frontier Territories, Diplomatic Zones
